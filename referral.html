Referrals

Make a referral to Reach.

Referring someone you support, or looking for support for yourself? Tell us what's needed in your own words. Anything you don't know yet, just write TBC — we'll work the rest out together. A real person will be back in touch within one business day.

    After you hit send

    What happens next.

    No black box — here's how a referral becomes supports that fit.

    01

    We read it properly

    Your referral and any documents land in our secure system, and a manager reads everything you've sent.

    02

    We get back to you

    Within one business day, to talk through what's needed, fill in any TBCs and check we're the right fit.

    03

    We meet them

    We arrive with their service agreement and plans already drafted from what you've told us, ready to go through together and change until they're right.

    04

    Supports start

    We match workers who suit them, confirm the schedule, and keep you in the loop as things get underway.
